# Cold-outreach templates — institutional leads, May 2026

**Audience:** the 17 priority institutional buyers tagged `edu-priority-lead` in Shopify.
**Purpose:** announce the Fall 2026 pilot + the new education-platform site to people who already bought from us once.
**Tone:** Andrey, founder, writing personally. No marketing copy. Short. One ask.

These are **warm leads**, not cold. Each person on this list already paid us money. Treat the email accordingly — referencing their actual order, not pitching from scratch.

## How to use this document

1. Each template is filled in for one specific lead. Copy / paste into Klaviyo or Gmail.
2. Subject lines under each header — pick one. The A variant is the safest; B is more direct; C tests humour.
3. Replace the `[OPTIONAL]` brackets with anything you remember about the relationship.
4. **Never** send all 17 the same day — Klaviyo will flag bulk-sends. Send 3-4 per day across one week.
5. The follow-up sequence at the bottom of this doc fires automatically 5 days then 14 days after, with no reply gates.

## Variables used in all templates

- `[FIRST_NAME]` — buyer's first name
- `[INSTITUTION]` — institution name
- `[LAST_ORDER_DESCRIPTION]` — one-line summary of their last order
- `[OUR_REPLY_TIME]` = "within one business day"

Send-from: `collaboration@playtronica.com` (Andrey).
Reply-to: same.

## Subject-line testing notes

- A subjects use the institution name early — strongest open rate for known relationships.
- B subjects use a number (€order amount, "three orders later") — works when the relationship has data behind it.
- C subjects are casual / direct — works for younger or maker-culture recipients (d.school, Drexel).

Rotate A/B/C across the 17 leads so no two adjacent emails use the same style.

## Best send times

- US K-12 / district: Tuesday or Wednesday, 9-10 AM their local time.
- US university: Tuesday-Thursday, 10-11 AM.
- EU institutional: Tuesday-Thursday, 10-11 AM their local time.
- Asia (HKBU, Singapore MOE): Tuesday-Thursday, 9-10 AM their local time.
- Never Monday (inbox triage) or Friday (already gone).

## What to do with replies

1. **Yes / interested** → reply within 1 business hour with a Calendly link. Block 30 min for the call, not 15 — 15 is too tight for an institutional conversation.
2. **Need more info / "send me a deck"** → reply with the [education.playtronica.com](https://education.playtronica.com) link + the relevant case study (Lincoln Center for K-12, Stanford for higher ed, Trossingen for EU conservatory). Don't send a deck — send the link.
3. **Procurement question** → reply with the relevant document directly attached (W-9, COI, DPA, etc.) and CC procurement@playtronica.com (if it exists) or admin@.
4. **No / not now** → thank them, ask if they know one colleague at another institution who might be interested, then move them to the `edu-warm-2027` tag for the spring cohort.
5. **No reply after 14 days** → leave them. Move tag to `edu-no-response-2026` so we can refine for the next cohort.

## Logging

Every reply goes into a single Klaviyo profile note (no spreadsheet). Klaviyo events to fire:

- `edu_outreach_sent` on send
- `edu_outreach_opened` on open
- `edu_outreach_replied_positive` on reply YES
- `edu_outreach_replied_negative` on reply NO
- `edu_pilot_application_submitted` on form submit
- `edu_call_booked` on Calendly hit
- `edu_pilot_selected` on shortlist
- `edu_pilot_shipped` on devices shipped

Weekly Klaviyo report: how many replies, how many calls booked, how many applications received — sent every Monday morning to Andrey.
