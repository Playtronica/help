## TL;DR — the headline

**This is not a green-field bet.** Playtronica already has 347 institutional orders (≥€500) in Shopify over the last 3 years from real institutions — Lincoln Center, Stanford d.school, Berklee, Hochschule Trossingen, Singapore MOE, Filharmonia Opolska. Phase-1 budget €40–80K is already approved internally. The strategic question is **not "should we?"** but **"why haven't we shipped the landing page yet?"**

The remaining decisions are **packaging and execution**, not strategy.

### Standards + procurement — US first, hard reqs

**Per-market launch order (from research):**

1. **US (Q3 2026, back-to-school)** — easiest path. NCAS HS Music Technology strand maps perfectly to Playtronica devices. FCC already done. Amazon Business EDU + Eduporium = 4-week distribution. ESSER funding context still alive but sunsetting fast.
2. **UK (Q4 2026)** — UKCA leverages CE, KS3 has explicit "music technology" requirement, CCS framework is centralized.
3. **DE (2027)** — Digitalpakt 2.0 funding through 2030 is biggest single EU tailwind, but DSGVO + AVV + e-invoice + 2-year warranty + Fachhandel-only adds 6–9 months of compliance.
4. **JP (2027–2028)** — requires PSE certification, local distributor (Korg Edu / MI7 / Roland Edu), GIGA-vendor whitelisting. Wait for inbound.

**Procurement auto-DQ red flags** (must NOT fail any of these):
1. No W-9 / no COI in US, or no AVV / no DPIA template in DE
2. Consumer-only 90-day warranty — need 1 yr US/JP, 2 yr EU/UK
3. No PO acceptance / credit-card-only checkout
4. Missing safety mark for jurisdiction (PSE for JP, UKCA after 2025)
5. Cloud account required with no DPA, or US-only data residency for EU

### 2. Name + primary tagline

- **"Skip the 45-minute tutorial. Playtronica gets every student making real sound on day one — standards-aligned lessons included."** (anti-buzzword, outcome-led)

### 3. Pricing locked (€390 / €1,380 / €3,690 / custom)?

Internal docs had €220 / €580 / €1,380 / €490. Research benchmarks push toward higher (€390 / €1,290–1,490 / €3,490–3,990). My proposal is between the two. Lock these numbers or tweak?

### 4. Lead capture mechanism — what flag controls the funnel?

Three options that compound:
- **A.** Quote form (already in `/orders/invoice-vat/` — repurpose).
- **B.** Free sample lesson PDF download in exchange for email + role + school name.
- **C.** Pilot program application form (limited to 5 schools, free in exchange for case study).

My recommendation: **all three** — A converts hot leads, B nurtures warm leads, C creates case studies. Which subset for Phase 0 (today)?

### 5. Pilot program — paid or free with credit?

- **Free** = more applications, lower quality, less commitment.
- **Free with €1,380 credit if they buy after** = filter for serious schools, still hooky.
- **Paid but discounted** = highest filter, slowest to fill.

My recommendation: **free + signed case-study consent + photo/video rights**. Real schools that need to be sold to other schools.

### 6. Sales human — who's the named contact?

The page needs ONE photographed human, named, with direct email + Calendly link. Three candidates from internal docs:

For Phase 0 (today): we need an existing human. Recommend Andrey or Aksinja. Hire later.

### 7. Cold outreach geography for first 200 leads

Internal docs said US-first. Research confirms. But you have institutional buyers in PL, DE, UK, JP, SG already. Three options:
- **US-only** (highest TAM, slowest cycle, ESSER funds sunsetting)
- **US + UK + DE** (Sonic Sales is your existing DE distributor — leverage)
- **Existing institutions first** (warmest leads, 80 already in Shopify — re-engage them)

My recommendation: **Existing 80 institutions first** (1 week of outreach, near-certain results) → **US-only second wave** (cold).

### 8. Subscription / recurring component — yes or no?

The investor benchmarks (8–12× ARR multiple) require recurring revenue. Three options:
- **No subscription** — pure hardware sales, simpler, lower exit multiple.
- **Annual curriculum subscription** — €99/year/school for new lesson plans + PD library access. Light touch.
- **Per-seat student subscription** — €5/student/year à la Soundtrap. Higher revenue, requires student accounts (privacy work, FERPA).

My recommendation: **Annual curriculum subscription €99/year/school**, layered on top of hardware. Light privacy work, real recurring revenue, matches Soundtrap entry-tier psychology.
