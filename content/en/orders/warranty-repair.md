Every Playtronica device has a **1-year warranty** against manufacturing defects, starting from the delivery date.

## What is covered

- The device stops functioning without physical damage or misuse.
- LED failure not caused by impact.
- USB port failure not caused by force or incorrect use.
- Component failure under normal use.

## What is not covered

- Physical damage from dropping or impact.
- Liquid damage. Biotron used with plants is an exception — that is expected use.
- Pin or component corrosion from improper storage, or from heavy use with acidic objects like citrus fruit.
- Damage from an incorrect power supply or incorrect voltage.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. [**Email support@playtronica.com**](mailto:support@playtronica.com) with the subject `Warranty #[order number]`. Describe the issue.
2. **Attach a short video or photos of the problem.** This helps us diagnose the fault. In most cases you will not need to ship the device back.
3. **We reply within 3 business days** with one of three outcomes: remote-fix instructions, a replacement part, or a full device replacement.

## Out-of-warranty repairs

The device is more than a year old, or the damage is not covered by warranty. We still want to help. [Email support@playtronica.com](mailto:support@playtronica.com) with the subject `Out-of-warranty repair #[order number]`. In many cases we can offer repair guidance, spare parts, or a discounted replacement.
