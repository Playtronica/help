You have **30 days** from the delivery date to return any Playtronica device for a full refund. The device must be:

- **Unused**
- In **original condition**
- In **original packaging**, with all cables and accessories included

## How to start a return

Write to **support@playtronica.com** with the subject `Return #[your order number]`. Tell us what you are returning and why.

We reply within 1 business day with the correct shipping address. We do not publish a single address because we route returns by region and product.

> ⚠️ **Do not ship returns to Estonia or to any address you find online.** Always wait for our confirmation email with the correct return address. Packages sent before we confirm may be lost in transit and we cannot refund them.

## If the device was a gift

The process is the same. In your email, include any of the following:

- Order number, if you can find it
- Email address used at checkout
- Recipient's name and shipping city

We will locate the order. If you have no order details at all, use the subject `Gift return` and include the recipient's name and delivery city.

## If the device arrived defective or broken

[Email support@playtronica.com](mailto:support@playtronica.com) with the subject `Defective #[order number]` and **attach a photo** of the issue. We handle each defect case individually. The most common result is a replacement device sent to you quickly.

> ⚠️ **Do not try to repair the device yourself before contacting us.** Repair attempts can void the warranty.

## If you ordered the wrong product

**If your order has not shipped yet**, we may be able to swap or cancel before it leaves the warehouse. [Email us](mailto:support@playtronica.com) immediately with the subject `Modify #[order number]` and tell us what you want changed. The warehouse ships within hours of checkout, so contact us as soon as possible.

**If your order has already shipped**, you can return the device under the 30-day policy and place a new order. We will process the refund as soon as the return arrives.

## When does the refund arrive?

We issue the refund **within 10 business days** of receiving your return at the warehouse. The money arrives in your account based on your payment method:

| Payment method | Time after refund is issued |
|---|---|
| Credit or debit card | 3 to 10 business days |
| Klarna, Apple Pay, Google Pay | Next business day |
| PayPal | 1 to 2 business days |

If 15 business days have passed since our "refund issued" email and you still do not see the refund, [email us](mailto:support@playtronica.com) with the subject `Refund check #[order number]`. We will trace the payment.

## What we cannot accept

- **Used or modified devices.** Cosmetic wear from normal use is fine. Rewired or soldered devices cannot be returned.
- **Returns missing components.** Include all cables, alligator clips, and accessories from the original box.
- **Returns past the 30-day window.** After 30 days, we review case by case. The warranty for manufacturing defects still applies — see [Warranty and repairs](/orders/warranty-repair/).
