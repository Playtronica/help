MIDI Marshmellow is a USB MIDI host. It passes MIDI from your Playtron, TouchMe, or Biotron to a hardware synth.

**MIDI only: it does not pass audio.**

## Connect your devices

Follow the [connection guide on the product page](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/midi-marshmellow%E2%84%A2).
Check your device connector before choosing a cable.

Power Marshmellow separately through the USB-C connector on top, using a 5 V USB charger or power bank.
