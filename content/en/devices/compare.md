Playtronica makes five MIDI controllers. Every one turns a different physical thing — skin, fruit, a plant, a rotating disc, a weight — into MIDI. They are designed to be played together as much as alone. This page is the long, honest comparison.

> 💡 **Not sure which device you already have?** That is a different page. See [Which Playtronica do I have](/getting-started/which-device/) — it identifies the device from a photo. This page is for picking which one to buy or play with next.

## One-line comparison

| Device | Plays itself? | What you do | Best partner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TouchMe** | No | Touch the device with your skin | Another person + TouchMe |
| **Playtron** | No | Clip 16 wires to fruit, water, foil, or skin | TouchMe in the other hand |
| **Biotron** | Yes — the plant plays | Plug into a houseplant | Headphones and a chair |
| **Orbita** | Yes — the motor plays | Place coloured magnets on a rotating disc | Any DAW or sampler app |
| **Scales** | No | Place an object on the plate | Other Scales devices |

## Pick by what you want to do

### "I want to play music with my hands the first time I plug it in"

→ **[TouchMe](/devices/touchme/)**. Touch the pads, it makes sound. No clips, no patches, no plant. The fastest cold-start in the line-up. Often the right gift.

### "I want to use real-world objects as instruments"

→ **[Playtron](/devices/playtron/)**. Sixteen alligator-clip pins map to sixteen notes. Clip them to fruit, water, foil, drawings made with conductive ink, your sister's hand. A workshop favourite.

### "I want music that plays by itself"

→ **[Biotron](/devices/biotron/)** if you want a living source: bio-signals from a plant become MIDI, light shapes the result. Generative and slow.

→ **[Orbita](/devices/orbita/)** if you want a mechanical source: a motor spins a magnetic pattern around a ring of "steps" and triggers MIDI as it rotates. Loop-based and rhythmic. Needs a synth or DAW connected.

### "I want a gestural, sculptural instrument"

→ **[Scales](/devices/scales/)** is the newest. Place an object on the plate; heavier objects play higher notes. Five modes turn that gesture into chords, arpeggios, tempo, or a tuning pad.

### "I want all of the above to play at once"

→ The **[All-in-One bundle](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/playtronica-all-in-one)** has every active device in one box. Or start with the **[Orbita + Biotron Duo](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/next-gen-playtronica-duo-orbita-biotron)** — Orbita as the rhythm, Biotron as the melody, no human input required.

## Still picking?

The shortest correct answer to "which one should I buy" depends on one question: **do you want the music to happen because you touched something, or do you want the music to happen on its own?**

- Touched something → **TouchMe**, **Playtron**, or **Scales**.
- On its own → **Biotron** (organic, generative) or **Orbita** (mechanical, loop-based).

Write to **support@playtronica.com** with the subject `Which device should I buy` and tell us briefly what you want to do — workshop, gift, installation, home studio, classroom. We reply within 1 business day with a recommendation.
