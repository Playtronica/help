Every Playtronica device works out of the box. The accessories on this page are optional — they extend what you can do, replace a part you have worn out, or smooth over the cable situation on your particular computer or phone. This page is the long version. Browse them all at [shop.playtronica.com](https://shop.playtronica.com).

> 💡 **Already own a device?** Most owners only ever buy two extras: a longer or replacement USB cable, and the [Accessory Kit](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/playtronica-accessory-kit) for installation work. Everything else is situational.

## Cables

Every device ships with the USB cable it needs. These are spares, replacements, longer runs, or the right end if you have a non-default port.

### USB-C to USB-A — 1 m

The default cable in the box for TouchMe, Playtron, and Orbita. This is the same cable in two color variants (white or matte).

- [USB-C to A — 1 m (matte)](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/usb-c-cable-1m) — €9.98
- [USB-C to A — 1 m (white)](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/cable-usb-c-to-a-1m-white) — €9.98

Buy a spare if you have lost yours or want one to leave permanently in your studio.

### USB-C to USB-A — 2 m

Longer run for installations, performances, or studios where the laptop sits across the desk. Otherwise identical to the 1 m version.

[USB-C to A — 2 m](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/usb-c-cable-2m) — €13.31

### USB-C to USB-C — 1 m

For modern laptops, iPad Pro, iPhone 15 and newer, and Android phones that only have a USB-C port. Plug the device straight into a USB-C port without an adapter.

[USB-C to C — 1 m](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/usb-c-cable) — €9.98

## Adapters

### USB Adapter for Android and older Mac

A 2-in-1 hood: one end is Micro-USB, the other end is USB-C. Plug it onto a USB-A device cable to land on an older Android or a Mac that only has USB-C ports.

[USB Adapter — 2-in-1](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/usb-adapter-2-in-1-micro-usb-and-usb-type-c) — €9.31

### iPhone Lightning Adapter

For iPhones and iPads with a Lightning port (iPhone 14 and earlier, plus older iPad models). The adapter exposes a USB-A port plus charging passthrough — so the iPhone keeps charging while a Playtronica device sends MIDI to GarageBand, Koala, or any other CoreMIDI app.

[iPhone Lightning Adapter](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/iphone-adapter) — €20.63

> 💡 **iPhone 15 and newer have USB-C** and do not need this adapter. Use the [USB-C to C cable](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/usb-c-cable) instead.

## Conductive materials

These let you build your own playable surfaces with Playtron and TouchMe. Stick them on paper, fabric, sculpture, walls, or 3D prints, and the surface becomes a sensor.

### Copper tape

8 mm wide, 10 m on a roll. Adhesive on one side, fully conductive top to bottom. Cut to length with scissors.

[Copper Tape](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/copper-tape) — €9.31

Use it for crisp, drawn-on surfaces — paper instruments, cardboard panels, wall installations, 3D-print contact points.

### Conductive fabric tape

Soft, bendable, adhesive. Works on textiles, garments, and curved sculpture surfaces where copper tape would crack. The same tape that ships inside the Accessory Kit.

[Conductive Fabric Tape](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/conductive-fabric-tape) — €11.31

## Patches and clips

### Patches for skin and plants — 4 pack

Stick-on conductive electrodes with an alligator-clip attachment point. Use them to clip TouchMe or Playtron to a comfortable spot on your skin without holding the device, or to attach Biotron's leaf-pads to a leaf without a good clip surface.

[4 Patches](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/patches-for-skin-and-plants) — €16.63

### Patches for skin and plants — 8 pack

Same patches, twice as many. Pick this size if you are kitting out a workshop, a body-music performance, or an installation that wears patches out fast.

[8 Patches](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/8-patches-for-skin-and-plants) — €26.62

### 10 extra alligator clips

Each Playtron ships with 8 alligator-clip cables. The 10-pack brings you to 18 — enough to use all 16 alligator-clip pins on Playtron at the same time, with two spares for when teeth break or wires bend.

[10 Extra Alligator Clips](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/10-extra-alligator-clips) — €9.31. Each clip is 44 to 50 cm long.
