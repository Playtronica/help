Playtronica sells a small set of accessories that extend what the devices can do. Every accessory is optional — none are required to play. This page tells you when each one matters.

Shop the full set at [shop.playtronica.com/collections/additional-items](https://shop.playtronica.com/collections/additional-items).

## Adapters

### USB-A to USB-C adapter (Android, older Mac)

If your computer has USB-A ports and your Playtronica device uses USB-C, you need this. Many Android phones with USB-C also need it for the other direction.

[USB Adapter — 2-in-1 Micro-USB and USB-C](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/usb-adapter-2-in-1-micro-usb-and-usb-type-c) — combo, covers both port types.

### iPhone Lightning adapter

iPhones with a Lightning port (iPhone 14 and earlier) need this to plug a USB-C Playtronica device into the phone.

[iPhone Lightning Adapter](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/iphone-adapter)

iPhone 15 and newer have USB-C and do not need this — use a standard USB-C to USB-A adapter or a USB-C cable directly.

## Conductive materials

These let you build custom playable surfaces with Playtron and TouchMe.

### Copper tape

8 mm wide, 10 m long. Adhesive on one side, fully conductive. Stick it onto paper, cardboard, wood, walls, or 3D prints. Each strip of tape becomes a Playtron-clippable surface or a TouchMe-touchable shape.

[Copper Tape](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/copper-tape)

### Conductive fabric tape

Bendable, soft, adhesive. Works on textiles, garments, and sculpture surfaces. Ships in the [Accessory Kit](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/playtronica-accessory-kit).

### Patches for skin and plants

Stick-on conductive patches with an alligator-clip attachment point. Use them to make a more comfortable skin contact than bare alligator-clip teeth, or to attach Biotron's leaf-pads to a leaf that does not have a good clip surface.

- [4 Patches for Skin and Plants](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/patches-for-skin-and-plants)
- [8 patches ship inside the Accessory Kit](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/playtronica-accessory-kit)

### 10 extra alligator clips

Each Playtron ships with 8 alligator-clip cables. The 10-pack gives you 18 in total — enough to use all 16 alligator-clip pins on Playtron at the same time, with two spares.

[10 Extra Alligator Clips](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/10-extra-alligator-clips) — each clip is 44–50 cm long.
