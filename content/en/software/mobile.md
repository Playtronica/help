> ⚠️ **Web MIDI does not work on mobile browsers.** Web MIDI is not supported on iOS or Android browsers. Use the apps listed below instead.

## Adapters you will need (not in the box)

- **iPhone or iPad (Lightning port).** Apple Lightning to USB Camera Adapter. Available from Apple for around €29. Cheaper third-party alternatives work.
- **iPhone or iPad (USB-C port).** Any USB-C to USB-A adapter. Modern iPads and iPhone 15 and later use USB-C.
- **Android phone.** A USB-C OTG adapter. Most modern Android phones support USB Host Mode. Check your phone's specifications first.

## How to connect

1. Plug the adapter into your phone.
2. Plug your Playtronica device into the adapter.
3. Open a MIDI-compatible app from the lists below. The app detects the device automatically.

## iOS apps

### Dawnbeat — recommended

Our top pick for Playtron on iPhone and iPad. Clean interface, excellent MIDI support. Good for live performance.

### GarageBand iOS — free

A full DAW. Supports external MIDI automatically. Has a large instrument library. Works well with TouchMe and Playtron. The drummer and keyboard tracks are a good starting point.

### Koala Sampler

Record any sound with your microphone, then play those samples from your Playtronica device. Fast, fun, and addictive. Free with in-app upgrades.

### AudioKit Synth One

A powerful open-source synthesizer. Good with TouchMe. Free.

## Android apps

### FluidSynth — recommended

Popular with our Android users. Select your Playtronica device at the top of the screen. Wide selection of instrument sounds.

### MidiKeyboard

Basic and reliable. A good choice for quick tests and verifying that the device is working.

### PerfectPiano

Simple and straightforward. The violin sound is especially good with plants and TouchMe.

### SynprezFM

More advanced FM synthesis. For users who want to explore sound design on Android.
