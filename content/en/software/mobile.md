> ⚠️ **Web MIDI does not work on mobile browsers.** Web MIDI is not supported on iOS or Android browsers. Use the apps listed below instead.

## Adapters you will need (not in the box)

- **iPhone or iPad (Lightning port).** Apple Lightning to USB Camera Adapter. Available from Apple for around €29. Cheaper third-party alternatives work. Playtronica also sells a [Lightning adapter](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/iphone-adapter).
- **iPhone or iPad (USB-C port).** Any USB-C to USB-A adapter. Modern iPads and iPhone 15 and later use USB-C. Playtronica sells a [USB-C / Micro-USB combo adapter](https://shop.playtronica.com/products/usb-adapter-2-in-1-micro-usb-and-usb-type-c) that covers both ports.
- **Android phone.** A USB-C OTG adapter. Most modern Android phones support USB Host Mode. Check your phone's specifications first.

## How to connect

1. Plug the adapter into your phone.
2. Plug your Playtronica device into the adapter.
3. Open a MIDI-compatible app from the lists below. The app detects the device automatically.

## iOS apps

### Koala Sampler — recommended

Record any sound with your microphone, then play those samples from your Playtronica device. Fast, fun, addictive. Free on the App Store with in-app upgrades. Strong choice for Playtron + TouchMe.

- App: [koalasampler.com](https://www.koalasampler.com/)
- Community: [Koala Discord + Facebook group + Twitch beatcast](https://www.koalasampler.com/community/) — active, friendly, lots of Playtronica setups in their feeds.

### Dawnbeat — Koala alternative

iPad and iPhone sampler very similar to Koala. Continuously records pad events so you can experiment freely, then save what you like. Strong MIDI support.

- App: [Dawnbeat on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dawnbeat/id6443720584)
- Web: [dawnbeat.com](https://www.dawnbeat.com/)

### GarageBand iOS — free

A full DAW. Supports external MIDI automatically. Large instrument library. Works well with TouchMe and Playtron. The drummer and keyboard tracks are a good starting point.

- App: [GarageBand on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/garageband/id408709785)

### King of FM

DX-style FM synth on iOS. Rich, classic sounds. Good with Orbita.

- App: [King of FM on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/king-of-fm-dx-synth-e-piano/id1672644102)

### AudioKit Synth One — free

A powerful open-source synthesizer. Good with TouchMe.

- App: [AudioKit Synth One on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/audiokit-synth-one-synthesizer/id1371050497)

### Elastic OSC — paid

Made by friends of Playtronica. Very good sound. Worth the price if you want a more cinematic palette.

- Web: [mominstruments.com/elasticosc](https://mominstruments.com/elasticosc/)

## Android apps

### FluidSynth — recommended

Popular with our Android users. Select your Playtronica device at the top of the screen. Wide selection of instrument sounds. Free.

### Koala Sampler — Android

Same app as iOS, same community. Strong choice on Android too.

- [koalasampler.com](https://www.koalasampler.com/)

### MidiKeyboard

Basic and reliable. A good choice for quick tests and verifying that the device is working.

### PerfectPiano

Simple and straightforward. The violin sound is especially good with plants and TouchMe.

### SynprezFM

More advanced FM synthesis. For users who want to explore sound design on Android.
