Every Playtronica device is a class-compliant USB MIDI device. No drivers are required on Mac or Windows. Plug in the device and the operating system recognises it automatically.

## On macOS

1. **Plug in the device** with a USB cable. Use a USB-C to USB-A adapter if your Mac has no USB-C port.
2. **Verify in Audio MIDI Setup.** Go to Applications → Utilities → Audio MIDI Setup. Click the MIDI Studio icon. Your Playtronica device should appear.
3. **Open your synth or DAW.** The DAW detects the device automatically. In Ableton, check MIDI preferences. In Logic, the device appears automatically.

## On Windows

1. **Plug in the device.** Windows 10 and Windows 11 install a generic MIDI driver automatically. No manual driver installation is required.
2. **Check Device Manager.** Right-click Start → Device Manager → Sound, video and game controllers. Your Playtronica device should appear in the list.
3. **Enable the MIDI input in your DAW.** In Ableton, go to Options → Preferences → MIDI. In FL Studio, go to Options → MIDI Settings. Enable the Playtronica device as a MIDI input.

## On iPhone or iPad

1. **Use a USB adapter.** You will need Apple's Lightning to USB Camera Adapter, or a USB-C to USB-A adapter for newer iPads. These are not included in the box.
2. **Plug the adapter into your iPhone or iPad, then plug in your Playtronica device.**
3. **Open GarageBand for iOS or Koala Sampler.** Both apps detect USB MIDI devices automatically. Create a new track and play.

## On Android

1. **Use a USB-C OTG adapter.** Most modern Android phones support USB Host Mode through OTG. Check your phone's specifications.
2. **Connect the Playtronica device through the USB-C OTG adapter.**
3. **Open a MIDI-compatible app.** Koala Sampler (Android) and MIDI+BTLE are good starting points. Allow the app to access USB devices when prompted.
