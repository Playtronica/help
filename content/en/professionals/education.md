Playtronica began in workshops. The first session was a music teacher letting children make sound from fruit and vegetables. Twelve years later, our devices are in classrooms, special education programmes, and university labs in more than 40 countries.

## Why Playtronica works in classrooms

- **No prior knowledge needed.** Any student of any age can make a sound within 30 seconds.
- **Bridges subjects.** A single activity connects music, science (electricity, biology), and art.
- **Inclusive and SEND-friendly.** Touch-based interaction works for students who cannot use traditional instruments.
- **Endless variation.** Sessions feel different every time because the "instruments" — plants, fruit, and objects — change.

## Which device for which age?

| Age group | Best device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 5–8 years | Playtron | Clipping wires to fruit is immediately engaging. Very visual and tactile. |
| 9–14 years | TouchMe or Playtron | Students can understand the science. Group play works very well. |
| 15+ and adults | Any device | Biotron opens up biology connections. All devices work. |
| SEND or sensory needs | TouchMe | Minimal setup, immediate feedback, and calming tones are possible. |

## Safety

All Playtronica devices run on **5 V USB power** — the same voltage as a phone charger. The electrical currents involved are far below any threshold of harm. Playtronica devices have been used safely in classrooms with children from age 4 for more than 10 years.

> ⚠️ **Pacemakers and other implanted medical devices.** Capacitive sensing and electromagnetic signals can interfere with implanted medical devices. Students or visitors with pacemakers or similar implants should not use Playtronica devices. See [Safety and health information](/professionals/safety/) for the full rules.
