## Festivals, exhibitions, and museum collaborations

- **[Centre Pompidou (Paris) — MИR: A Journey into Space and Sound](https://www.centrepompidou.fr/en/program/calendar/event/ceEdzRg)** (summer 2018) — month-long installation covered by [FactMag](https://www.factmag.com/2018/06/28/playtronica-centre-pompidou-exhibition/) and [TYPICA](https://typica.mu/en/entry/57ae9ce2-fbe7-4f16-9614-e9016811629d).
- **[Garage Museum of Contemporary Art (Moscow) — workshop "The Rhythm of Color"](https://garagemca.org/en/event/workshop-with-playtronica-the-rhythm-of-color)** — early prototype work that later became Orbita.
- **Palais de Tokyo (Paris)** — ongoing collaboration on contemporary-arts workshops.
- **Tretyakov Gallery (Moscow)** — sound installation programme.
- **Exploratorium (San Francisco)** — interactive station for the science-museum audience.
- **Sónar+D (Barcelona)** — Playtronica was founded in 2014 after a visit to the first Sónar+D, and has shown at the festival across multiple editions.

## For press enquiries

Write to **collaboration@playtronica.com** with the subject `Press — [outlet name]`. We reply within 2 business days with high-resolution photography, a one-paragraph studio bio, and (if relevant) a loan device for review. Hands-on review units go out to reviewers with verified outlets and a stated publication window.
