The Playtronica community is the first place to ask for help. Other users have hit your problem before, and the answers stay searchable for the next person.

## Where to go

[**facebook.com/groups/playtronica**](https://www.facebook.com/groups/playtronica) — public group, **4400 members** active across 40+ countries. The Playtronica Friends community on Facebook.

**Best for:** finding past answers, posting a new question that anyone can find later, sharing what you made, asking about creative use and installations.

> ℹ️ **We are looking for a better long-term home for the community.** Facebook works for now, but if you have suggestions for a better platform, [email support@playtronica.com](mailto:support@playtronica.com) with the subject `Community suggestion`.

## How to ask a good question

The same advice works for any channel.

1. **Search first.** In the Facebook group, click the magnifying glass and try terms like `playtron mobile no sound`, `all leds lit`, `touchme biotron no sound`, `koala sampler midi`.
2. **Add a `[Device]` prefix** to your post title. Example: `[Biotron] Same notes repeat without touching the plant`.
3. **In the post body, include:**
   - Which device — TouchMe, Playtron, Biotron, Orbita, or Scales.
   - Your operating system — macOS, Windows, iOS, Android, or a hardware synth.
   - The app you are using — synth.playtronica.com, Ableton, Logic, GarageBand, FL Studio, Koala, Dawnbeat, or another.
   - What you have already tried.
   - What is happening. A short video or photo helps a lot.

A clear question gets faster answers. A vague question rarely gets answered at all.

## When to email support instead

The community is great for many things but it cannot help with these. **Email [support@playtronica.com](mailto:support@playtronica.com)** for:

- Order tracking, missing items, or shipping questions.
- Returns and refunds.
- Invoice and VAT requests.
- Warranty claims and broken devices.
- Anything that needs your order number or your shipping address.

For business and bulk orders, use [collaboration@playtronica.com](mailto:collaboration@playtronica.com).

The Playtronica team aims to reply to email within 1 business day. Allow up to 3 business days at busy times.

## How the community works

The community is run by the Playtronica team in Berlin and Paris. The admin is Andrey. The community manager checks the group several times a day and seeds firmware updates, new device announcements, and FAQ posts. Other members answer help questions — that is what makes the community a community.

The group has three rules:

1. **Share your wonderful creations.**
2. **The group is not for solving delivery issues.**
3. **Be kind and courteous.**

The first one is the most important. Post what you make. Other people will be inspired.
